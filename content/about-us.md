## What is Orbit?

Orbit is a powerful Discord bot designed to make server management easier, smarter, and more enjoyable. From moderation tools to leveling systems and custom automations, Orbit gives server owners full control over their community.
With a integrated web dashboard you can configure the whole bot, from a to z!

## Our Mission

We believe every Discord community deserves a reliable and feature-rich bot without the complexity. Our goal is to build tools that are easy to set up, simple to use, and powerful enough to grow with your server.
